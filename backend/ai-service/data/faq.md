# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Orders
**Q: How do I place an order?**  
You can place an order by adding products to your cart and proceeding to checkout.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**  
Orders can be canceled within 2 hours of placing them.

## Payments
**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**  
We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and net banking.

**Q: Is Cash on Delivery available?**  
Yes, Cash on Delivery is available for selected locations.

## Account
**Q: Do I need an account to order?**  
No, you can checkout as a guest.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions.
